About me

A bit about
who I am

Hello, I am Piyush Chauhan, a passionate Computer Science Engineering student @REC MAINPURI and an undergraduate in the Bachelor of Science in Data Science and Applications program at @IIT Madras based in India. Driven by curiosity and a love for building things, I enjoy exploring the intersection of software development, data science, and creative technology.

Whether I am writing clean code, engineering logic to solve complex problems, or working on full-stack web projects, I am constantly pushing myself to learn and adapt. Beyond the screen, I have a deep appreciation for the arts particularly sketching and listening music. I believe that balancing technical logic with creative expression is what makes building for the web and software truly exciting.
